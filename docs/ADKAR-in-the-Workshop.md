# ADKAR Throughout the Learning Journey

The workshop integrates principles from the Prosci ADKAR Model to support sustainable AI adoption.

Rather than presenting ADKAR as a standalone theory, the workshop embeds each stage within practical learning activities. Participants experience the change process while developing AI capability.

The objective is to help learners move from understanding AI to applying it confidently within their own work.

---

# Awareness

## Understanding Why AI Matters

The workshop begins with participants analysing their own workplace tasks.

Rather than asking which AI tools are available, participants first consider:

- Which tasks consume the most time?
- Which activities are repetitive?
- Where are delays, bottlenecks, or inefficiencies?
- Which tasks require significant human judgement?

This creates awareness by connecting AI to genuine business needs rather than technology trends.

---

# Desire

## Seeing Personal and Organisational Value

Participants explore task-level AI recommendations using structured reports and facilitated discussion.

Together they examine:

- opportunities for automation
- opportunities for augmentation
- tasks requiring human control
- potential risks
- expected benefits

Because participants work with their own professional context, they begin to identify improvements that are personally meaningful and organisationally relevant.

---

# Knowledge

## Learning Practical AI Skills

Participants gradually develop the knowledge required to support responsible AI adoption.

Activities include:

- interpreting task analysis reports
- selecting appropriate AI tools
- designing prompts
- evaluating AI outputs
- documenting workflows

Learning is built through guided exploration rather than instructor-led demonstrations.

---

# Ability

## Applying Learning to Real Work

Participants immediately apply new knowledge by creating AI-supported workflows based on authentic workplace activities.

Throughout the workshop they:

- refine prompts
- evaluate AI responses
- discuss alternative approaches
- improve workflow designs
- receive facilitator feedback

Ability develops through repeated practice within realistic workplace scenarios.

---

# Reinforcement

## Supporting Continued Adoption

The final stage focuses on sustaining change beyond the workshop.

Participants leave with practical workplace resources including:

- task analysis reports
- prompt documentation
- workflow designs
- AI opportunity assessments
- personal adoption plans

These outputs encourage continued experimentation, discussion, and improvement after formal learning has ended.

---

# The Facilitator's Contribution

The facilitator supports each stage of ADKAR by creating an environment where participants:

- analyse rather than memorise
- discuss rather than simply observe
- experiment rather than follow fixed procedures
- reflect before making implementation decisions

This approach encourages confidence, critical thinking, and responsible AI adoption.

---

# ADKAR as a Learning Framework

Within this workshop, ADKAR is not treated as an additional topic.

It provides the structure that connects learning activities with organisational change.

Each workshop activity contributes to building awareness, developing capability, and supporting sustained workplace adoption.

The result is a learning experience that focuses not only on using AI, but on integrating AI into everyday work in a practical, responsible, and sustainable way.
