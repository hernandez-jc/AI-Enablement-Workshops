# Bespoke AI Enablement Engagements

Every organisation approaches AI from a different starting point.

Some are exploring AI for the first time.

Others have already introduced AI tools but are looking for practical ways to integrate them into everyday work.

For this reason, the workshop is designed as a flexible framework rather than a fixed training programme.

The methodology remains consistent while activities, examples, and learning resources are adapted to each organisation's work, objectives, and operating environment.

---

# A Flexible Framework

The workshop can be adapted to different:

- occupations
- business functions
- organisational priorities
- levels of AI maturity
- governance requirements
- learning objectives

Participants always begin with their own workplace tasks before exploring AI opportunities.

This ensures that learning remains relevant regardless of role or industry.

---

# Examples of Adaptation

## Finance and Accounting

Activities focus on:

- financial reporting
- budgeting
- forecasting
- compliance
- audit preparation
- document-intensive workflows

Discussion emphasises responsible AI use, verification, governance, and human oversight.

---

## Insurance

Examples may include:

- claims processing
- underwriting support
- policy documentation
- customer correspondence
- knowledge retrieval
- workflow consistency

Participants evaluate where AI can improve efficiency while maintaining regulatory and organisational requirements.

---

## Human Resources

Participants analyse activities such as:

- recruitment
- onboarding
- policy documentation
- employee communications
- performance support

The discussion explores where AI can assist and where empathy, judgement, confidentiality, and organisational policy remain essential.

---

## Operations and Professional Services

Examples focus on:

- documentation
- reporting
- workflow design
- knowledge management
- internal communication
- administrative processes

Participants identify opportunities to improve consistency, reduce repetitive work, and strengthen decision support.

---

# Supporting Different Levels of AI Maturity

The workshop can support organisations at different stages of AI adoption.

### Early Exploration

Participants develop awareness, understand AI opportunities, and begin experimenting with practical workplace applications.

### Growing Adoption

Teams evaluate existing AI practices, improve prompting techniques, redesign workflows, and introduce consistent working methods.

### Organisational Integration

Activities focus on sustainable adoption, governance, responsible AI, and embedding AI within everyday work processes.

---

# Working Alongside Existing Technology

The workshop does not promote a particular AI platform.

Instead, participants learn principles that can be applied using the AI tools already approved by their organisation.

This allows the learning experience to complement existing technology strategies rather than replace them.

---

# Facilitation Rather Than Prescription

Every engagement begins by understanding the organisation's work.

Examples, discussions, activities, and workplace tasks are selected to reflect participants' professional context.

The facilitator guides exploration, encourages discussion, and supports evidence-based decision making rather than presenting fixed answers or one-size-fits-all solutions.

---

# The Intended Outcome

The objective is not simply to improve AI skills.

The objective is to help organisations build practical capability to evaluate AI opportunities, redesign workflows responsibly, and support sustainable adoption within their own operating environment.

That is why every engagement begins with work.

Technology follows.
