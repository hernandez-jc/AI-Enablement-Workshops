# Workshop Deliverables

The workshop is designed to produce practical workplace outputs rather than temporary learning experiences.

Each activity contributes to a tangible deliverable that participants can continue using after the workshop.

These outputs encourage immediate application, support organisational learning, and provide a foundation for responsible AI adoption.

---

# Why Deliverables Matter

Many AI training programmes finish with demonstrations or prompt exercises.

This workshop finishes with practical resources that participants can adapt, refine, and apply within their own work.

The emphasis is not simply on learning AI.

The emphasis is on improving work.

---

# Participant Deliverables

## Task-Level AI Opportunity Analysis

Participants analyse occupational or organisational tasks using structured reports that identify opportunities for:

- Automation
- Augmentation
- Human Control
- Mixed / Split Workflows

These reports become the foundation for discussion and workflow design.

---

## AI Opportunity Assessment

Participants identify where AI can provide meaningful value while recognising situations where professional judgement, governance, or organisational policy should remain central.

The objective is informed decision making rather than maximum automation.

---

## Prompt Development Log

Participants document prompt iterations throughout the workshop.

Rather than recording a single "perfect prompt," they compare different approaches, evaluate results, and refine their prompts through experimentation and feedback.

This creates a reusable prompt library grounded in authentic workplace tasks.

---

## AI Output Evaluation

Participants evaluate AI-generated outputs against practical criteria such as:

- accuracy
- completeness
- relevance
- organisational context
- potential risks
- verification requirements

This reinforces responsible AI use and critical thinking.

---

## AI-Supported Workflow

Each participant designs at least one AI-supported workflow for a real workplace activity.

The workflow identifies:

- where AI contributes
- where human judgement is required
- review and verification points
- expected business benefits

The result is a practical workflow that can continue to evolve after the workshop.

---

## Personal Adoption Plan

Participants finish the workshop with a simple implementation plan describing:

- one workflow to apply
- expected benefits
- potential barriers
- organisational support required
- next steps

This helps bridge the gap between learning and workplace adoption.

---

# Organisational Value

Although participants create individual deliverables, the outputs can also provide value to organisations.

Collectively, they help organisations:

- identify AI opportunities across work tasks
- understand where human oversight remains essential
- capture practical workflow improvements
- encourage consistent AI practices
- support future learning initiatives

The workshop therefore contributes not only to individual capability but also to organisational learning.

---

# Learning Through Authentic Work

Every deliverable is based on authentic workplace activities.

Participants work with their own tasks whenever possible.

This ensures that workshop outputs remain relevant, immediately applicable, and aligned with organisational objectives.

Rather than leaving with notes from a training session, participants leave with practical resources that support continued AI adoption.
